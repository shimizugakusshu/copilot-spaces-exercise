# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a customer-focused, iterative approach to project management. Projects are delivered through small, testable increments, with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous improvement.

This directory provides a central reference for OctoAcme's project management processes, team responsibilities, and delivery workflows.

## Project Management Lifecycle

OctoAcme organizes project delivery into five main stages:

1. **Initiation:** Identify the business problem, establish success metrics, align stakeholders, and approve the initiative for planning.
2. **Planning:** Define scope, prioritize the backlog, estimate work, identify dependencies, and establish milestones and acceptance criteria.
3. **Execution and Tracking:** Deliver work incrementally, monitor progress, conduct reviews, manage blockers, and validate quality.
4. **Release and Deployment:** Confirm release readiness, complete testing, deploy changes, verify functionality, and communicate results.
5. **Retrospective and Continuous Improvement:** Review outcomes, document lessons learned, and track improvement actions.

Risk management and stakeholder communication continue throughout the project lifecycle.

## Key Workflows

### Planning and Delivery

- Begin approved initiatives with a project kickoff.
- Maintain a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria.
- Track work using a project board with stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done.
- Deliver small, testable increments through pull requests.
- Run automated tests and lint checks before requesting code review.
- Obtain the required review approval before merging.

### Communication and Progress Tracking

- Use regular team standups and delivery syncs to discuss progress, blockers, and dependencies.
- Review project risks and update the risk register during weekly syncs.
- Provide stakeholder updates according to project needs and agreed communication schedules.
- Escalate unresolved issues through the PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor as appropriate.

Meeting frequency may vary by team agreement and project requirements.

### Release and Quality Management

- Confirm acceptance criteria, CI checks, and security scans before release.
- Prepare release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans.
- Verify deployments and communicate release outcomes.
- Record incidents and follow up on corrective actions.

### Continuous Improvement

- Conduct retrospectives after sprints, releases, significant milestones, or incidents.
- Identify successful practices and opportunities for improvement.
- Assign owners and deadlines to improvement actions.
- Track follow-up actions through the project backlog or issues.
